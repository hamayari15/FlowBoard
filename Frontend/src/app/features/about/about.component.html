FAQ

Questions people ask before they start

Is FlowBoard free to use?

Yes — core boards, tasks, and team members are free. We'll introduce paid plans for larger teams later, but nothing you build today will be locked behind a paywall.

Can I invite my whole team?

Yes. Add teammates to a board and everyone sees updates in real time — no separate licenses or seats to manage per board.

Does it work for Scrum, not just Kanban?

Both. Use a simple column board for Kanban-style flow, or group tasks into sprints with dates and burndown tracking if your team runs Scrum.

What happens to my data if I delete a task?

Every change — including deletions — is recorded in the activity log first, so you can always trace what happened to a task before it's gone for good.
