Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's how FlowBoard collects, uses, and protects your information.

Introduction

Welcome to FlowBoard. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we keep it secure when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you create a FlowBoard account, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and password. We also collect the content you create on the platform, including boards, tasks, comments, and attachments, so we can provide the service to you and your team.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to operate and improve FlowBoard, including syncing your boards in real time, sending notifications about task updates, and helping you and your teammates collaborate. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Protection

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We restrict access to your information to authorized personnel who need it to maintain and support the platform, and we regularly review our security practices to keep your boards safe.

4. Cookies

FlowBoard uses cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how the platform is used so we can improve it. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work correctly if you disable them.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services for things like hosting, analytics, and email delivery. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function and are required to protect your data in line with this policy.

6. Data Retention

We keep your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and maintain your account. When you delete your account, we take reasonable steps to remove your information from our active systems, except where we are required to retain it for legal or security purposes.

7. Account Security

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential. We recommend using a strong password and avoiding sharing your login information with others. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

You can access, update, or delete your account information at any time from your FlowBoard settings. You may also request a copy of your data or ask us to permanently delete your account by contacting us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services or legal requirements. When we make significant changes, we will provide appropriate notice and update the policy on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, reach out to us at privacy@flowboard.com and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
